## Headline numbers

- At ndim=4 the equivariant bases stay at **20 columns** against PDE-FIND's **280** and WSINDy's **211**.
- Build time at ndim=4: multigraph 24 min, mod. multigraph 31 min, PDE-FIND 74 min (3.0x).
- Peak memory at ndim=4: multigraph 2.0 GB, PDE-FIND 3.2 GB (1.5x).
